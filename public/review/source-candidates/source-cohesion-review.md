# Water 9 Source Cohesion Review

Generated: `2026-06-18T17:07:20.458Z`

This board exists to prevent prototype screenshots from being treated as production art. Automation packages evidence; only a human can approve cohesion.

## Policy

- Prototype screenshots do not count toward the strict 20-threat gate.
- Human source approval is required before production rigging or threat acceptance.
- Every approved source must include specific notes for every required cohesion check.
- A passing sandbox/source preview is evidence, not approval.

## Cohesion-First Principles

- **Full-source concept first** `full-source-concept-first`: Approve the creature only after the intact source image reads as one coherent animal or organism before any limbs, parts, or animation frames are extracted.
- **Mechanical rig preview is not art approval** `mechanical-preview-is-not-art-approval`: A sandbox screenshot, contact sheet, or working articulation proves technical readiness only; it cannot rescue source art that lacks a unified silhouette, anatomy, palette, and material language.
- **Reject placeholder cohesion** `reject-placeholder-cohesion`: If the design reads as placeholder parts, unrelated pasted fragments, or a proof-of-concept assembly, reject it for regeneration rather than promoting it into the 20-threat gate.

## Required Checks

- `whole-creature-cohesion`: The image reads as one designed organism, not a bundle of unrelated shapes.
- `part-continuity-cohesion`: Limbs, joints, materials, lighting, and proportions remain continuous across riggable parts.
- `readable-silhouette`: The creature is identifiable at gameplay scale and has a clear attack profile.
- `no-collage-artifacts`: No pasted, mismatched, duplicate, or style-inconsistent fragments are visible.
- `non-placeholder-art-direction`: The art looks like production source material, not a temporary technical prototype.
- `clean-magenta-key`: The pure magenta background can be removed without white/dark halos or lost anatomy.
- `neutral-riggable-pose`: The pose exposes pivots, attack parts, and crop-safe margins for articulation.
