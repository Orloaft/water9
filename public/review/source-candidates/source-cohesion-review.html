Water 9 Source Cohesion Review

Prototype-vs-production boundary for generated source art. Automation packages evidence; only a human can approve cohesion.

candidates 20 ready 20 human approved 0 prototype locked 20 evidence complete 20

Policy

Prototype screenshots do not count toward the strict 20-threat gate. A passing sandbox preview is evidence, not approval. Production rigging requires human source approval with specific cohesion notes.

npm run source:cohesion-review && npm run source:cohesion-review-check
npm run source:approval-runway && npm run source:approval-runway-check

Cohesion-First Principles

  • Full-source concept firstfull-source-concept-firstApprove the creature only after the intact source image reads as one coherent animal or organism before any limbs, parts, or animation frames are extracted.
  • Mechanical rig preview is not art approvalmechanical-preview-is-not-art-approvalA sandbox screenshot, contact sheet, or working articulation proves technical readiness only; it cannot rescue source art that lacks a unified silhouette, anatomy, palette, and material language.
  • Reject placeholder cohesionreject-placeholder-cohesionIf the design reads as placeholder parts, unrelated pasted fragments, or a proof-of-concept assembly, reject it for regeneration rather than promoting it into the 20-threat gate.

Required Cohesion Checks

  • Whole creature cohesionwhole-creature-cohesionThe image reads as one designed organism, not a bundle of unrelated shapes.
  • Part continuity cohesionpart-continuity-cohesionLimbs, joints, materials, lighting, and proportions remain continuous across riggable parts.
  • Readable silhouettereadable-silhouetteThe creature is identifiable at gameplay scale and has a clear attack profile.
  • No collage artifactsno-collage-artifactsNo pasted, mismatched, duplicate, or style-inconsistent fragments are visible.
  • Non-placeholder art directionnon-placeholder-art-directionThe art looks like production source material, not a temporary technical prototype.
  • Clean magenta keyclean-magenta-keyThe pure magenta background can be removed without white/dark halos or lost anatomy.
  • Neutral riggable poseneutral-riggable-poseThe pose exposes pivots, attack parts, and crop-safe margins for articulation.
