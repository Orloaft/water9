# Blunt visual inspection

## Passes

- Canonical A read survives: large cyan faceplate, silvered-brass dome, copper dorsal mass, broad brass cuirass, black bellows, heavy gloves and split fins all remain readable at the live 43 px-wide footprint.
- Frames read as painterly authored silhouettes, not vector blocks, procedural primitives, traced pixel art, palette swaps, or puppet cutouts.
- Limbs and hands remain connected in all seven runtime frames. Scanner intent is visibly two-handed in deploy/hold/recover before the modular runtime cone is considered.
- Hover, propulsion, cruise, deploy, hold, and recover are visually distinct. No v2 pixels or geometry were reused.
- Runtime alpha has transparent corners, binary values, zeroed transparent RGB, and shows no magenta fringe or dark matte halo in upper, mid, or lower canvas evidence.
- The localized visor beacon and warm/cool material split survive both color and grayscale boards.

## Caveats

- This is a deliberately tiny motion gate: two-frame locomotion loops read as authored key alternation, not finished fluid animation. The next production gate needs hand-authored in-betweens.
- Normalizing separately authored masters into a common cell creates a small amount of shoulder/helmet registration breathing between propulsion and cruise. It is acceptable for concept-motion review but should be onion-skinned and corrected before expanding the clip.
- Fine dents and fittings collapse into broad clusters at runtime, intentionally. Some copper/brass texture still flickers between frames because the source paintings are individually authored; a dedicated shared-material repaint should precede final atlas approval.
- The existing modular scanner cone partially overlaps the new scanner head in live play. The body/tool gesture remains readable, but effect origin/scale should be tuned at the next gate.
- Left-facing runtime uses Phaser mirroring. This motion test does not claim the bible-required left-facing light/asymmetry repaint.
- Deep diagonal rotation is inherited from the live swim pose code; it remains readable, but the two-frame loop has not yet been reviewed at every pitch/facing combination.

## Gate verdict

PASS for manager review as a bounded Concept A motion-test and live bitmap-loading proof. It is not approval of a complete diver replacement package.
