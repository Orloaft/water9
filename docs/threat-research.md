# Water 9 Threat Research Briefs

These briefs are source-art inputs for new articulated threats. Each candidate
should still begin as one cohesive magenta-background whole-creature image before
any part extraction or rigging work begins.

## Cavitation Boxer

Prototype registered as `cavitation-boxer`; it still needs visual acceptance before
counting toward the 20-threat goal.

A smasher mantis-shrimp threat with two oversized folded club appendages,
stalked eyes, plated body, and a fan tail. It should read as a compact armored
brawler whose danger spikes only after a clear wind-up.

Biological anchors:

- Smasher stomatopods, especially peacock mantis shrimp, with blunt dactyl clubs
  instead of spear claws.
- Spring-loaded raptorial limb logic: arms tuck under the head, then snap out.
- Cavitation as the signature effect: a white-blue bubble burst or shock ring at
  the club tip after the punch.
- Stalked compound eyes and bright meral warning spots for readability.

Articulatable parts:

- head/carapace plate
- 3 overlapping abdomen shell segments
- tail fan/uropods
- left and right eye stalks
- optional antennae
- each punch arm split into upper merus/shoulder, folded forearm/propodus, and
  round dactyl club
- grouped underside legs
- separate VFX for cavitation flash, expanding bubble ring, and water shear

Motion phases:

1. Idle guard: clubs tucked, eyes scanning, tail pulsing.
2. Telegraph: body compresses backward, clubs pull tight, warning spots flash.
3. Strike: one club snaps forward; body lunges slightly.
4. Cavitation: bubble bloom appears at club tip, then expands as a ring.
5. Recovery: club overextends and creates a punish window.
6. Reset: arm folds back under the head.

Gameplay verb: bait the punch, dodge the shock, punish recovery. The attack is
short-range with medium-radius knockback or stun, strong telegraph, very fast
release, and slow recovery.

Image prompt seed:

```text
2D game sprite source sheet creature, cohesive underwater enemy called Cavitation Boxer, smasher mantis shrimp inspired stomatopod, side view facing right, long armored segmented shrimp body, high stalked compound eyes, fan tail, two oversized folded raptorial boxing-club appendages under the head, pale mineral hammer caps, vivid teal green orange violet shell markings, bright meral warning spots on inner arms, clean readable silhouette, parts visually separable for later rigging and cropping, crisp painterly sprite art, no outline clutter, full creature centered, neutral pose with clubs cocked, solid pure #ff00ff magenta background
```

Prompt risks:

- Avoid humanoid gloves, shorts, faces, or sports motifs.
- Avoid spear claws; this must be a smasher with blunt clubs.
- Avoid too many tiny legs or photoreal detail that ruins crop readability.
- Generate cavitation VFX separately so bubbles do not overlap the base body.

## Sand Battery

Prototype registered as `sand-battery`; it still needs visual acceptance before
counting toward the 20-threat goal.

A stargazer-inspired buried electric ambusher that reads as a sand mound until it
charges. It uses cheek electrocyte pads, shovel fins, a venom spine, and a hinged
mouth to punish careless approaches.

Biological anchors:

- Stargazer fish: upward eyes, upturned mouth, buried ambush posture, and venomous
  spine cues.
- Electric fish: oversized biological electrocyte pads and blue-white discharge
  glow, not metal batteries or wires.
- Sand-diving flatfish cues: broad shovel pectorals, camo fringe, short tail, and
  a low mound silhouette.

Articulatable parts:

- root body / buried mound
- short tail stub
- far and near pectoral shovel fins
- sand skirt / camo fringe
- head plate
- hinged jaw lip
- eye bank
- near and far electrode pads
- dorsal venom spine
- separate shock ring/cone VFX for gameplay, not baked into the base source

Motion phases:

1. Idle buried: tiny body pulse, eye scan, pectoral fins sift sand.
2. Sense: eye bank rises, electrode pads alternate glow, skirt trembles.
3. Charge: body compresses upward, jaw cracks open, venom spine lifts.
4. Discharge: electrode pads flare and external shock VFX fires.
5. Recover: glow fades while the exposed body can be punished.
6. Rebury: fins shovel and tail shimmies until the mound settles.

Gameplay verb: spot the mound, bait the charge, dodge the pulse, punish the
cooldown. Contact or spine hits can apply venom slow; the electric discharge is
telegraphed and strongest in front of the head.

Image prompt seed:

```text
Flat pure #ff00ff magenta background, isolated full-body 2D game sprite source art, cohesive underwater enemy called Sand Battery, stargazer-inspired buried electric ambusher, side-facing three-quarter view facing right, squat flattened fish body adapted for hiding in sand, huge upward-angled grim mouth, small high-set eyes, sandy armored back plates, broad shovel pectoral fins, short tail, thick biological electric whisker/barbel cables, oversized blue-white electrocyte pads along cheeks fins and back, gloomy abyssal sand predator, crop-friendly separated fins tail jaw eye bank electrode pads and venom spine, crisp painterly browser-game sprite, no environment, no shadow, no text
```

Prompt risks:

- Avoid a literal AA battery, wires, metal electrodes, machinery, or sci-fi tech.
- Avoid fully buried source art; the rig needs readable head, torso, fins, tail,
  sockets, and crop margins.
- Avoid top-down pancake art; use right-facing three-quarter side view.
- Keep shock rings, sand plumes, and electric arcs separate from the base source.
- Keep pure magenta only in the background.

## Siphon Lily

Prototype registered as `siphon-lily`; it still needs visual acceptance before
counting toward the 20-threat goal.

A stalked ambush predator with a sea-lily or pitcher silhouette: flared cup-body,
two hinged anemone petal jaws, siphonophore bead organs, and a few broad stinging
ribbons.

Biological anchors:

- Carnivorous anemones: oral disc, central mouth, retractile tentacles, and
  cnidocyte sting logic.
- Siphonophores: modular colony organs represented as pulsing bead-zooids.
- Tunicate/vase-sponge forms for the cup and throat silhouette.
- Crinoid or sea-lily cues only as graceful silhouette garnish.

Articulatable parts:

- root/base anchor
- 3-4 segmented stalk pieces
- main pitcher cup/throat
- left and right petal jaws
- optional rim/lip piece
- inner siphon tongue or throat valve
- grouped rim tentacle fringe
- 3 broad stinger ribbons with bulb tips
- lure filament with glowing bead
- 4-6 dorsal siphonophore bead organs

Motion phases:

1. Idle pump: cup inflates/deflates; beads pulse; ribbons drift.
2. Lure: bead flicks forward; tentacles curl inward.
3. Telegraph: cup opens wider; throat glows; suction begins.
4. Siphon: player/particles drift toward mouth; stalk leans into pull.
5. Snap: petal jaws close into a bud; ribbons sweep inward.
6. Recover: closed bud pulses, spits bubbles/debris, then reopens.

Gameplay verb: siphon-pull then snap. Counterplay is to break lateral alignment,
dash behind the stalk, or bait the snap with a projectile or decoy.

Image prompt seed:

```text
Flat pure #ff00ff magenta background, isolated full-body 2D game sprite source art, no environment, no shadow, no labels. Creature design: Siphon Lily, cohesive underwater ambush predator, marine animal not terrestrial plant, stalked pitcher-shaped sea anemone with two large hinged lily-petal jaws around a dark siphon throat, translucent teal and jade body, cream rim, violet outline, coral-pink inner mouth not matching magenta background, four translucent siphonophore bead organs along the back, one glowing lure filament, three broad trailing stinging ribbons, small grouped tentacle fringe around the rim, side-facing three-quarter view, readable silhouette at 64px, bold clean contours, crop-friendly separated appendages with visible hinge points, stylized browser-game sprite, crisp painterly detail, centered with margin
```

Prompt risks:

- Avoid terrestrial leaves, soil, or a literal pitcher plant.
- Avoid becoming a jellyfish; the stalked cup must dominate.
- Avoid too many fine tentacles; use 3-5 broad ribbons.
- Keep pure magenta only in the background.

## Harpoon Cone

Prototype registered as `harpoon-cone`; it still needs visual acceptance before
counting toward the 20-threat goal.

A slow cone-snail ambusher that behaves like an organic underwater speargun. It
waits or creeps half-buried, extends a proboscis, then fires a venom barb on a
readable tether line.

Biological anchors:

- Predatory cone-snail body plan: patterned cone shell, visible foot, aperture,
  siphon, extendable proboscis, and hollow venom tooth.
- Hook-and-line fish-hunting behavior adapted into a narrow telegraphed shot.
- Organic chitin harpoon, not a metal weapon.

Articulatable parts:

- shell body
- soft foot skirt
- aperture lip
- siphon feeler
- proboscis base, mid, and tip segments
- harpoon tooth
- venom glow at barb/tip
- runtime tether line effect

Motion phases:

1. Idle/buried: shell rocks, foot barely visible, siphon samples water.
2. Detect: aperture darkens, siphon aims, shell pivots.
3. Aim telegraph: proboscis snakes out; venom glow builds.
4. Strike: harpoon snaps forward in 1-2 frames.
5. Tether/paralyze: target slows or stuns; thin tether remains visible.
6. Retract: proboscis contracts in segments and may tug the target.
7. Reload: barb disappears, aperture closes, shell hunkers down.

Gameplay verb: respect the line. The player watches for the proboscis aim,
dashes perpendicular, hides behind terrain, or punishes during cooldown.

Image prompt seed:

```text
Flat pure #ff00ff magenta background, isolated full-body 2D game sprite source art, cohesive underwater enemy called Harpoon Cone, predatory cone snail inspired marine gastropod, three-quarter side view facing right, large patterned triangular cone shell, dark crescent aperture at front, pale muscular foot skirt visible underneath, short siphon feeler, oversized crop-friendly segmented proboscis extended forward, translucent organic chitin harpoon tooth at the proboscis tip with small cyan-green venom glow, bold readable shell bands and spots, separated appendages with visible hinge points for later articulation, crisp painterly browser-game sprite, no environment, no shadow, no text
```

Prompt risks:

- Avoid a generic empty shell; require visible foot, aperture, siphon, and
  proboscis.
- Avoid a metal spear; specify organic translucent chitin tooth.
- Avoid a proboscis too thin to crop; ask for readable game-sprite proportions.
- Avoid ocean scenery or shadows; require flat magenta background.
